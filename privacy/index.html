Cabin Music — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Summary

Cabin Music does not collect or send user data to developer servers.

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. Information used for playback is stored in the app's private storage on your device. When you export a backup or playlist, the app writes a file to the location you choose.

The app has an optional paid feature set (PRO). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never receives your payment method or card details.

What the app accesses

Cabin Music plays music stored on your device. It uses these permissions to do so:

PermissionPurpose
Read audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO) List and play the music on this device
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Show playback controls
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK) Keep playing while the app is not on screen
Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK) Stop the device sleeping mid-track
Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) Apply the equaliser, bass boost and virtualiser
Delete files (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below) Only when you choose to delete a track from within the app
In-app billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) Buy PRO through Google Play and check whether it has been bought
Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Added by the media playback library. The app plays files on the device and makes no network connections

Playback permissions are used on the device. Import and export access the file or location you choose through Android's file picker.

What the app stores

Cabin Music keeps the following in the app's private storage:

  • Favourited tracks, groups you created, and any order you arranged by hand
  • Titles, artists, album names and cover colours you corrected yourself
  • Playback settings (sort order, repeat, shuffle, speed, equaliser)
  • Display settings (theme, language)
  • Play counts, recent playback times, and the last playback positions for tracks and groups
  • Lyrics you enter or read from audio files
  • Whether PRO is unlocked (the result of checking your Google Play purchases)

This lives in the app's private storage, unreadable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Backup and moving to a new device

The music library database is excluded from automatic cloud backup. This applies to database entries such as groups, favourites, lyrics and listening history.

Carried over on a direct transfer. On Android 12 and above, the data does travel when you move it straight from an old phone to a new one. That transfer happens between the two devices and is not stored on a server along the way. On Android 11 and below, a cloud backup and a direct transfer cannot be told apart, so the data travels on neither.

On the new device, what was stored is matched back against the music that phone holds. Anything that names a track the new phone does not have stays unmatched rather than being applied to a different song.

Your chosen language means the same thing on any device and is backed up in both cases.

Manual exports are saved to the location you choose. A backup can contain folder paths, filenames, groups, favourites, edits, lyrics, listening history and settings. An M3U8 playlist contains paths to audio files. Neither export includes the audio files themselves. If you choose a cloud provider such as Google Drive, that provider may store the file under its own policies. The app does not upload it automatically.

Third parties

Cabin Music contains no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting.

The only third-party component is the Google Play Billing library, used for the PRO purchase. It talks to the Google Play Store app on your device, and Google processes the payment under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Cabin Music does not receive payment method details. Google Play tells the app whether the purchase has been made, and it is restored on a reinstall or a new device with the same Google account. The developer can see the sales reports Google Play Console provides.

Children

Cabin Music collects no personal data from any user, so there is nothing collected from children either.
